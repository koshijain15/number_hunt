HOW SCORING WORKS

Every guess gets scored against the secret number: C (Correct) is how many digits are in exactly the right position. W (Wrong) is how many digits exist in the number but in the wrong position. Neither counts digits that aren't in the secret at all.

Example: if the secret is 1234 and you guess 1325, digit "1" is correct-position (C), digits "3" and "2" are in the number but wrong position (W), and "5" doesn't appear at all.

FIND AI'S NUMBER

The AI picks a secret number. Type your guess into the boxes and submit — you'll see C/W feedback and your guess gets logged in the table below. Keep narrowing it down until C equals the number of digits.

I GUESS YOUR NUMBER

Think of your own secret number (don't tell me!). I'll guess, and you tell me the C/W feedback using the small digit boxes. I use your feedback to narrow down the possibilities each round.

RACE MODE

You and the AI each try to guess a number — you guess the AI's number, then score the AI's guess against a number you're implicitly playing against. Turns alternate. Whoever reaches full marks (C = number of digits) first wins.

PLAY A FRIEND ONLINE

Create a room to get a 4-letter code, or join a friend's code. Both of you privately set a secret number, then guess each other's at your own pace — no turn order. First to crack it wins.

USING THE DIGIT BOXES

Tap a box and type — it auto-advances to the next box. Backspace steps back. Left/Right arrows move between boxes without erasing. Enter submits from any box.

STATS & RANKINGS

The left menu (☰) shows your personal games played/won. This panel's Rankings tab shows the global leaderboard across everyone who plays — sorted by games won.
